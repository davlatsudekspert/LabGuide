## 2. If the Uzbek edition is made to fit: option A (longer scenes) versus option B (cut words)

* **Option A - re-render the Uzbek editions with longer scenes.** Extra seconds = sum over lines of the overrun; 'natural speed' keeps the voice as synthesised, '+12 % tempo' lets ffmpeg speed every long line up by 12 %. Upper bound: a re-timed layout could reuse the slack of short lines, so the real need lies between the sum and about half of it. The English/Russian editions keep their length; the Uzbek video would be longer than the English one.
* **Option B - keep the video, cut words.** Words that must go so that every line fits at +12 % tempo (characters over the cap / average word length of the line).
