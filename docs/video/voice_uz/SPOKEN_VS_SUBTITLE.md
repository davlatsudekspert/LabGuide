# Voice text versus on-screen subtitle (spoken-Uzbek-only)

Owner's rule from listening tests: the UzbekVoice `shoira` voice cannot read English/Latin terms and letter-by-letter respellings ('pe de ef', 'e ge ef er') sound bad. So the **voice text contains only ordinary Uzbek words**; the **on-screen subtitles are unchanged** and keep PDF, eGFR, GC-MS, mmol/L ... (normal in dubbing: the voice says the Uzbek equivalent while the screen shows the abbreviation). Numbers are always spelled in Uzbek words. Status: **nobody has listened, no TTS was called**; every row is NEEDS_NATIVE_REVIEW. Generated by `python3 tool/tts/plan_uz.py` from `tool/tts/lexicon/spoken_uz_only.json`; reusable glossary: `GLOSSARY_UZ_ONLY.csv`.

Replacement order used: (a) the standard Uzbek/Russian-lab term, (b) a short descriptive Uzbek phrase, (c) dropped from the voice only when the sentence stays correct without it, (d) personal names and the brand: plain Uzbek spelling, owner approval.
