# LabGuide overview — claims → evidence and epistemic status

Status of the whole video: **EXPERT_REVIEW_PENDING**. Nothing here is marked as approved, verified by an expert, certified or validated.

**What is on screen.**
* **Hook:** original motion graphics (paper sheets, chat bubbles, a calculator). No real document, chat or product; the file name and chat texts are invented for the joke and carry no data. The "= ?" on the calculator is not a computed result.
* **Everything after the hook:** real app frames of the **free app** `/home/user/wt-audit` (commit `215d3e8`), filmed by `video_studio/flutter_walk/overview_walk_test.dart` and merged by `video_studio/scripts/overview_frames.py` with frames reused from earlier topic videos (list in `storyboard.json → data.reuse`: calculators, qc, calibration, leukocyte, photometry — same app commit, same walk kit).
* **Class screens** (class code and QR, assign test, results, student join, "assigned tests" card) were filmed with the app's own **widget-test fake server** (`test/helpers/fake_backend.dart`). The class name, the three students ("Student 01–03") and their scores are **demo data**. The badge says "Real app screens · demo class data".
* **No numbers** are used for the app's size (no counts of cards, decks, questions or users). The only numbers on screen are ones the app itself shows in its own screens (for example "5 questions · 10 min", the demo scores, "Today's 5 questions").
* **Not shown, not mentioned:** paid or subscription features, prices, trial, "Pro", partner ads, the new Auto slide mode. `wt-paid` differs from `wt-audit` only by additive Pro entry rows behind `BuildFlags.proEnabled` (off by default) and the Auto slide mode in `lecture_screens.dart` / `lecture_present.dart`; none of that appears in a frame (frames are from `wt-audit`).
